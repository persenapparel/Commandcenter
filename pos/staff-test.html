EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING

Test your badge and PIN.

Each employee uses their own email and password. Do not use the owner's sign-in. This page tests credentials only and never opens the cash register.

Connecting to secure staging…

1 · Your staging account

If you already have a confirmed staging account, sign in. Otherwise create one with the **same email the owner entered** for your badge. You must confirm the email before activation.

Need a new account?

Confirm the email you receive. The owner must then activate your reserved employee badge before the PIN test can pass.

2 · Scan your badge

After your separate account has been activated by the owner, scan the 8-digit employee badge and enter your employee PIN. No owner PIN is needed here.

A successful test confirms your cashier credentials only. Sales, discounts, refunds and owner settings are not enabled on this test page.
