Automatic receipts: when a connected print station is armed on your Windows computer, every completed cash sale is queued to that printer, even when the cashier uses a phone or iPad. Keep the station open. You can still print a manual backup below.

How the Dell + NELKO receipt station works
  1. Connect the NELKO to your Dell by USB, install its official driver, and choose the correct paper size.
  2. Open the POS Print Station page in Chrome on the Dell, sign in, print a test receipt, and select Start automatic printing.
  3. Keep the Dell awake, connected to the internet, and Chrome open. After a confirmed cash sale, the print station checks the receipt queue and asks the NELKO to print.
  4. Tear off the receipt. If nothing prints, inspect the printer before manually retrying the job to avoid duplicates.

This page cannot verify that the Dell is online. Silent printing needs a configured Chrome kiosk-printing shortcut; the NELKO paper/driver still needs a physical test.
